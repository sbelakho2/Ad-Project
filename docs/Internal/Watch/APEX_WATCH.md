# Setup Watch

Setup Watch is free. It is the daily public check of one publisher's
app-ads.txt and mediation setup: monitoring, the read-only audit and the
app-ads.txt watch run every day for every connected publisher. There is no
daily watch plan, tier, checkout, payment or renewal.

## Sign-up and confirmation

`/watch/` collects the domain, work email, optional network account ids and
optional store app lines. A confirmation email is sent; the link is inert
until the confirmation POST consumes it. One submission creates one pending
subscriber; a repeat inside ten minutes reuses the live token without sending
a second email. After confirmation the watch is active and free.

## What the daily pass does

The runner checks the publisher's app-ads.txt authority and daily source
rows, records findings, and sends alerts for failed pulls, expired keys,
metric drops and app-ads.txt identity loss. Monitoring never emits an
invoice line.

## Results fee

If the publisher later connects accounts and keeps an optimized change,
Apex keeps 10% of players on the old setting and bills 30% of the monthly
gain that holdout measures. A month with zero or negative measured gain bills
nothing; a confidently harmful change is rolled back; a change bills for at
most twelve months. The signed holdout result and ledger references back
each fee line.

## Legacy data

Older subscriber rows may contain `billingProfile`, `paymentLink` or tier
fields. They are read only for compatibility and never produce a charge.
