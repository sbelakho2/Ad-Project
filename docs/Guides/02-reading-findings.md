# What each finding means

Every finding carries a severity, a subject and the numbers behind it. Here is
what the rules report and what to do about each.

## Stale floor

A country floor is set on an ad unit, yet that pair earned nothing while it
still served impressions. The floor is blocking demand. The remedy is to lower or
remove the floor and let the platform prove the change.

## Missing app-ads.txt line

A network that is enabled in your account is not listed in your app-ads.txt.
Authorized sellers are read from that file, so a missing line can leave demand
on the table. The remedy is one line in the file on your developer website.

## Reporting gap

The platform total and the mediation total disagree by more than the tolerance.
This is usually a measurement problem, not a pricing one. Check the reporting
dates and the account ids before acting on it.

## Severity

Critical and warning findings are the ones worth a change. Info findings are
context. A change is only proposed from a finding that the free audit can back
with numbers, and every change is proven by the platform experiment before it
is billed.
