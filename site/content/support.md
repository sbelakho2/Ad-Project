For audit access, platform connections, experiment status or billing, email [hello@apexmediation.ee](mailto:hello@apexmediation.ee). Include your publisher ID, the relevant date and any report, experiment or invoice reference.

Leave keys, tokens and full credentials out of the message.

## A running test needs attention

Describe the affected app or ad unit and the last status you can see. If the console reports a failed restore, quote the incident reference.

## A fee needs explaining

Open the invoice calculation and include the invoice and change references.
