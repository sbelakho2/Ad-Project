We use the contact and portfolio details you submit to arrange an audit and respond to you. When you connect a platform, the service processes the account identifiers, configuration, reporting and experiment information needed for the agreed work.

Stored platform credentials are encrypted. Relevant records are used to operate the service, verify changes and calculate fees. Ad requests continue through your existing serving setup.

Contact [hello@apexmediation.ee](mailto:hello@apexmediation.ee) to ask about your information or request access, correction or deletion. We will explain any records that need to be retained for an active service, billing or another applicable obligation.
