## Good settings do not stay good forever.

Country floors, bidder settings and revenue reports need regular attention. Apex checks the configuration you have, identifies issues worth reviewing and gives each finding an evidence trail.

## Know where to look first.

The audit checks stale floors, disabled bidders, missing country floors, reporting gaps and app-ads.txt issues. Each finding names the affected scope and shows the data behind it. Recovery estimates appear only where the available data supports a model.

## Audit. Review. Test. Decide.

Begin with read access. Review a proposed change before granting write access. Where the platform and deployment support it, test the change in a platform-native experiment. Keep or stop it under rules set before the test begins.
