A proposal states the current and test settings, affected scope, allocation, burn-in, analysis window and stop conditions. New accounts start with approval required. Your approval applies to that proposal and its registered rules.

When supported and authorized, the runner creates the platform experiment. Apex evaluates finalized data under the registered rule. Pending data means waiting. A guardrail can stop or invalidate the test.

A failed test does not create a lift fee. A stop or restore is shown as confirmed only after the platform outcome is known. If a restore fails, the incident remains visible and needs intervention.
