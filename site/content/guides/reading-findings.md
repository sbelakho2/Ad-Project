A stale-floor finding identifies a floor and the reporting behind it. A missing-floor or disabled-bidder finding identifies the relevant setting. A reporting gap compares matched source totals. An app-ads.txt finding identifies the file, line or expected seller that needs review.

Critical findings can block platform changes. An estimate is a modeled opportunity, not measured lift. Review dates, scope and missing inputs before proposing a test.
